What is Communication?

Communication is the ability to receive messages from a high spiritual source, beyond information visible to the eye. The messages can come through sensations, thoughts,

images, words, dreams, visions, or one of the five spiritual senses, and their purpose is to help a person understand their situation, refine their path, and gain clarity on matters that concern them.

What is Jewish Kabbalistic Communication?

  Jewish-Kabbalistic communication is based on the wisdom of Kabbalah, the Zohar, the writings of Kabbalists and Chassidic teachings.

The communication is conducted through connection to the Creator and according to what is given from Heaven at that moment. Sometimes the messages can also come through righteous souls and angels, but everything is subject to the Creator's will and Heaven's decision.

The purpose is not fortune telling, but rather receiving guidance, understanding the root of matters, connecting to the soul and refining one's path.  

How are the messages received?

  The messages can come in various ways:

- Inner sensations

- Sudden thoughts and insights

- Images or symbols

- Dreams

- Visions

- Inner hearing

- Clear inner knowing

Sometimes only part of the picture arrives and sometimes a broader picture, according to what is right and needed at that moment.
