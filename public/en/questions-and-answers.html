Questions and Answers

Answers to frequently asked questions about Jewish Kabbalistic communication and the spiritual process

  Communication is the ability to receive messages, insights and guidance from a higher spiritual source, beyond information visible to the eye. The messages can come through sensations, thoughts, images, words, dreams, visions or one of the five spiritual senses, and their purpose is to help a person understand their situation, refine their path and gain clarity on issues that concern them.  

  Jewish-Kabbalistic communication is based on the wisdom of Kabbalah, the Zohar, the writings of Kabbalists and Chassidic teachings.

The communication is done through connection to the Creator and according to what is given from Heaven at that moment. Sometimes the messages can also come through righteous souls and angels, but everything is subject to the Creator's will and Heaven's decision.

The purpose is not fortune telling, but rather receiving guidance, understanding the root of matters, connecting to the soul and refining one's path.  

  The messages can come in different ways:

- Inner feelings

- Sudden thoughts and insights

- Images or symbols

- Dreams

- Visions

- Inner hearing

- Clear inner knowing

Sometimes only part of the picture arrives and sometimes a broader picture, according to what is right and needed in that moment.

A communication session usually lasts about an hour and a half. Sometimes one session is enough to receive meaningful insights, but many women choose to continue for several sessions to deepen the spiritual process. There's no obligation to commit to a series of sessions — each person decides according to her heart's feeling and personal need.

The best preparation is to come with an open heart and pure intention. It's recommended to dedicate time to inner quiet before the session, to think about the questions that occupy you, and perhaps even write them down. No special preparations are needed beyond that — simply come as you are, with the desire to connect to your soul.

Deep spiritual guidance through Kabbalistic wisdom — a journey into the soul, to discover the light within you and live a life of wholeness and true meaning.
